What we do

From the roadside to the recommendation.

Most transport questions stall somewhere between the data that was never collected and the analysis nobody can read. We work across that whole span — designing the collection, building the tooling, and delivering the answer.

Transport surveys

Roadside interviews, household and hotel travel diaries, public transport and parking interviews, special generator studies. Six digital instruments built for field teams, so responses arrive structured and ready to analyse.

Counting and flow analysis

Real-time vehicle counting and traffic flow analysis from video, for road usage studies, scheme appraisal and monitoring — without a clipboard at every junction.

Analysis and appraisal

Accessibility gaps, trip generation, origin–destination patterns, park-and-ride behaviour. Analysis scoped to the decision in front of you, with the working shown.

Visualisation and web tools

Interactive maps, network animations and dashboards that make a transport network legible to a room full of stakeholders — and stay online long after the report is filed.

Why the moving dot

A city is not a place. It is a pattern of movement.

Every journey in a network is one dot, moving. Follow enough of them and a city explains itself. That idea runs from the name through to the way we work — and, at greater length, through a six-part reflection on the dot itself.
