About

Planners who build software

The Moving Dot Ltd is a transport software and consultancy company. We started from a simple frustration: transport studies end in a report, while the data, the model and the tools behind them are left behind.

So we do both. The same team that frames the planning question designs the surveys, models the network and builds the software that keeps the answer usable after the project closes.

We have delivered work in the UK, Saudi Arabia and the UAE, and work with clients worldwide.

No hand-off

The person who understands the planning question is the person building the tool.

Software that stays

You keep working dashboards and platforms, not just a PDF.

Data you can defend

Every cleaning and validation step is recorded, so the numbers stand up to review.
